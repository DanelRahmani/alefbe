الفبا — Audio File List
=======================
Place all files inside a folder named "data/" next to alefba.html.
Letter audio goes in  data/audio/
Word audio goes in    data/audio/words/

When files are ready, uncomment the two audio blocks in alefba.html:
  1. In playLetterAudio() — for letter sounds
  2. In speakWord()       — for dictionary word sounds
